# 01: Explore a first French Revolution event in English and French

## What to build

Deliver the first usable slice of a simple educational website: secondary-school students can enter an explorable timeline, select the opening crisis of 1789, and understand its causes and significance in English or French. The completed experience should support an approximately five-minute overview of 1789–1799. Students explore freely rather than following a mandatory guided sequence.

Existing local work is an unfinished, unverified draft and is not committed to the GitHub repository. Treat it as reference material if available; the ticket must be implementable from this description alone. Website implementation and deployment are future work, not part of ticket creation.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The opening screen introduces the French Revolution and offers an obvious way to reach the timeline.
- [ ] Selecting the Estates-General event displays its date (5 May 1789), place, a short explanation of debt, bread prices and representation, and why it matters.
- [ ] Students can switch between English and French; navigation, explanatory text, dates and accessible labels change together while the selected event remains selected.
- [ ] Navigation supports selecting events directly as well as previous/next controls, with clear boundaries and a visible selected state. Adding further events does not require replacing the interaction model.
- [ ] The experience works with keyboard controls and on a narrow mobile screen, without clipped essential content.
- [ ] The event links to a reliable historical source, such as Château de Versailles, and uses age-appropriate language.
- [ ] Verify the first-event selection and language-switching flows in a browser.
