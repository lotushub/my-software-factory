# 04: Experience the timeline as an accessible cinematic story

## What to build

Give the freely explorable educational timeline the student's chosen cinematic character: dramatic historical imagery, a coherent visual atmosphere, and meaningful animated scene transitions. Keep short explanations readable and all navigation usable on mobile devices.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The opening composition uses appropriate historical imagery, strong typography and an obvious timeline entry point. Artwork is licensed for reuse and attributed; do not present a painting as depicting events it does not show.
- [ ] Event changes produce restrained animated transitions that connect a selected timeline point with its explanatory scene.
- [ ] Reduced-motion preferences remove nonessential motion while retaining complete content and interactions.
- [ ] Desktop and narrow mobile layouts keep English and French copy, navigation, selected events and controls readable and reachable without overlapping or clipping.
- [ ] Keyboard focus is visible, controls have usable touch targets, contrast is sufficient, and essential information is available without hover.
- [ ] Images are appropriately sized and a readable fallback remains if optional fonts or imagery fail to load.
- [ ] Browser-check the opening and timeline at desktop and approximately 360px mobile widths in both languages, including reduced-motion mode.

## Blocked by

- 01: Explore a first French Revolution event in English and French.
